Elvison – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy describes how Elvison ("we", "us") handles data in Elvison Ads Manager, an internal tool our team uses to manage advertising campaigns and Pins for our own business and for clients who have authorized us.

1. Who we are

Elvison, elvison.com. Contact: roelof@elvison.com.

2. Data we access

When an authorized account holder connects a Pinterest account via OAuth, we access only the data needed to provide the service: ad account details, campaigns, ad groups, ads, Pins and boards owned by that account, and advertising performance metrics. We do not access data of other Pinterest users.

3. How we use data

  • Creating, editing, pausing and resuming campaigns, ad groups, ads and Pins on behalf of the account holder.
  • Uploading creative assets supplied by the account holder.
  • Reporting campaign performance to the account holder.

We do not sell data, use it for advertising profiling, or share it with third parties except service providers needed to run the tool (hosting), bound by confidentiality.

4. Storage and security

OAuth access tokens are stored encrypted and used only to perform actions requested by the account holder. Access is limited to authorized Elvison team members.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep tokens and data only while the connection is active. You can revoke access at any time in your Pinterest settings (Security → Connected apps) or by emailing us; we then delete stored tokens and associated data within 30 days.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data, or object to processing, by contacting roelof@elvison.com.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
